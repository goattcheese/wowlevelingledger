      Rested XP

      Rested XP doubles kill XP until it runs out, so the most rested pair levels fastest. Set where each character logged out and how much rested it had left; the ledger works out how much it has now. An inn or capital city fills 5% of a level every 8 hours, outdoors a quarter of that, up to 150%. A Camp Tent tops you up to 5% straight away. Rates are Classic values until Forever publishes its own; the in-game addon will fill this in exactly.

          Sync with the Duo Ledger addon

          From the game: after a /reload or logout, press Sync from game and pick DuoLedger.lua in WTF\Account\<account>\SavedVariables. You can also paste the addon's Export text below. Characters are matched by name, so name them on the Pairs tab exactly as in game. Syncing the same data twice is safe, and a session that grew since last time is updated. The import also raises levels, sets hearths, marks dungeon quests turned in and wishlist items obtained.

          To the game: press Send to game and save LedgerData.lua into Interface\AddOns\DuoLedger, replacing the old one. Then type /reload in game. This works while you're playing, because the Ledger and the game never write the same file.

          Export format for addon authors (version 1)

          JSON with app "ForeverDuoLedger" and v 1. "characters" is optional; restXP, xpMax, resting and logout there fill the Rested tab; professions (skill by name) and inventory (bags plus bank, item name to count) fill the materials planner; "sessions" holds one entry per character per session. Times are ISO 8601. Quests are matched by name and items by item id. "mail" lists what that character mailed; entries that match the Mailbox queue by recipient name and item name are marked sent. Optional "kills", "moneyGained" (copper) and "deathLog" feed the Stats tab. A top-level "addons" list ticks installed addons on the Launch prep tab, and every character found by name is marked as reserved.

          Each pair puts one character from each of you together. The tank side favours Warrior, Paladin, Druid, Warlock and Shaman early and saves Priest for last. The healer side leads with the four healing classes, then tanking on Warrior.
          
          
          Within a pair no primary profession repeats, each character places one campsite object that is not cancelled by a class buff in the pair, and armor or stat priorities differ so drops are rarely contested. The checks under each pair recompute when you change anything.

          If you swap at level 20 or 30
          
        
          Rows are the tank side's options, columns the healer side's. Each cell shows how the two would share loot and whether they can start in the same zone. The plan levels a replacement up to the swapped pair's level, so any three-star cell is a safe swap.
          
      
          
      
          
      
          
        
          Common swap paths
          
        
            
          
          • Paladin + Priest (A) to Warrior + Druid (B): both are zero-overlap pairs, so you can also cross them (Paladin + Druid, Warrior + Priest) and stay clean.
            • 
          
          • Bear + Shaman (C): if the bear stalls, Warrior + Shaman is the best rescue; Tauren, Troll, Orc and Skyborne all allow both.
            • 
          
          • Shaman tank (D): the riskiest tank on the list. At 40 dual spec lets the healer side go Protection on the Paladin while the Shaman goes Enhancement or Restoration.

          Campsite objects
          
        
          Each primary profession unlocks its first object at 20 skill (free from the Camping 101 quest in the starting zones, or the trainer). First Aid and Fishing objects are open to every character. A buff from a camp object is mutually exclusive with the matching class buff, so the pair grid below flags any object that would be wasted.

          Campfire basics
          
        
            
          
          • Basic Campfire (Cooking 1) holds 3 objects; Journeyman (Cooking 90) holds 5; Expert (Cooking 220) holds 10.
            • 
          
          • The Journeyman Campfire blueprint drops in Wailing Caverns (and Deadmines). Cookie's Feast, a Stamina food object, drops from Cookie in Deadmines.
            • 
          
          • Buffs last one hour, and each camp feature has a one hour personal cooldown after use. Sitting, lying down or kneeling near the fire for about a minute applies them; you can now cast and craft while you wait.
            • 
          
          • Campfires no longer burn characters below level 5 (Oct 1 build).

          Legacy points
          Each of you earns up to 65 Legacy Points on your own account, from classes, tradeskills, PvP, dungeons and raids. Every character can spend 16 of them, so plan the build per character. Talented moves talent points as early as level 5 and Well Rested boosts rested XP; both feed the Talents and Rested tabs here. Professions and Resourcefulness perks help crafters.

          Leveling map
          
        
          Each pair follows one of two questing routes. The Eastern Kingdoms Route quests mostly on the eastern continent (Silverpine, Hillsbrad, Arathi, Stranglethorn, Badlands, the Plaguelands). The Kalimdor Route quests mostly on the western continent (Barrens, Ashenvale, Desolace, Feralas, Tanaris, Un'Goro). Both include the new Riverglades zone, and the Kalimdor Route also covers Shen'dralas. Pairs alternate routes so back-to-back pairs see different quests; dungeons stay on the shared checklist either way. Pick a pair to see its starting zone and route numbered in order.
          Each Alliance pair follows one of two questing routes. The Eastern Kingdoms Route (Alliance) runs Westfall, Redridge, Duskwood, the Wetlands, Hillsbrad, Arathi, Stranglethorn and on through the Badlands and Hinterlands to the Plaguelands. The Kalimdor Route (Alliance) runs Darkshore, Ashenvale and Stonetalon, a Wetlands stop, then Desolace, Dustwallow, Feralas, Tanaris, Azshara and Un'Goro. Both include the new Riverglades zone, and the Kalimdor Route also covers Shen'dralas. Boats link Menethil Harbor with Auberdine and Theramore, and the tram links Stormwind and Ironforge. Pick a pair to see its starting zone and route numbered in order.

              Suggested ground rules
              
          
                
            
              1. Paired characters only earn XP together. Solo time goes to professions, auction house, fishing or travel. If one of you slips a level ahead, the other catches up before the next session.
                2. 
            
              2. Swap checkpoints at 20 and 30. At each, spend five minutes asking whether both of you still enjoy the pair. If not, pick a three-star cell from the swap matrix and level the replacement to match while the other benches.
                3. 
            
              3. Level 40 is the lock. Train riding, set up dual spec (open world plus dungeon), and commit to 60.
                4. 
            
              4. Quests before dungeons. Check the dungeon card a level early and grab every pickup; most Horde dungeon quests start in a capital or a nearby outpost.
                5. 
            
              5. Queue when the lower level hits the quest level, not the Group Finder minimum. Many dungeon quests need a level or more above it (Ruins of Lordaeron wants 16, Razorfen Kraul wants 30 for all its queststhe Deadmines wants 16, Gnomeregan wants 28 for all its quests). The Quest-ready check on the Dungeons tab does the math from your levels.
                6. 
            
              6. Split the pickups. When a dungeon's quests start in different cities, each of you collects the shareable ones in one city and you share them when you meet. The Tonight tab writes the split for you; quests marked Not shareable or Own drop still need both of you.
                7. 
            
              7. Loot funnel. In your own runs, anything neither current character can use goes to the right alt. In group finder runs with other players, roll greed for alts rather than need.
                8. 
            
              8. Camp at every logout. Whoever has a Camp Tent drops it before you log; that is free rested XP. Rotate who hosts so the other's object can fill the third slot.
                9. 
            
              9. One crafter per material early. Until each side has several characters past 40, mail bars, leather and cloth to whichever character holds the matching crafting profession.
                10. 
            
              10. Mark new findings here. Notes under each pair are shared, so record quest pickups and drops that the guides missed.

              What the Oct 8 build changed
              
          
                
            
              • Creature XP in dungeons is up about 20%.
                • 
            
              • Party XP for killing lower-level enemies now uses each player's own level, not the highest in the party. A level gap in a duo no longer cuts the lower player's XP.
                • 
            
              • City of Dalaran dungeon is open for testing (no level range given, so the ledger's 28 to 33 stays).
                • 
            
              • Desolace Gelkis and Magram quests need level 35, and their 16 rewards will get a level 35 equip requirement in a hotfix.
                • 
            
              • Some dungeon quest rewards dropped from Rare to Uncommon with adjusted stats. Blizzard did not say which, so the wishlist data is unchanged until the new values are confirmed.
                • 
          
              
          
              What the Oct 1 build changed
              
          
                
            
              • Level cap raised to 30. Excavation Site: Wetlands opened (listed 26 to 31, now matching the dungeon list), plus Razorfen Downs and Uldaman.
                • 
            
              • Dungeon quests now give 50% less bonus XP, so they still pay but less than before.
                • 
            
              • Warriors gain extra rage from basic attack crits: the first Oct 1 notes said 75%, and the Warrior update added that evening raised it to 100%. Rend and Sunder tag mobs instantly; Sunder threat corrected. Protection moved Focused Rage earlier and removed Toughness.
                • 
            
              • Bear Form and Dire Bear Form gain 75% more rage on crits, Primal Bite threat roughly doubled, Swipe now scales with attack power, and Tiger's Fury is gone.
                • 
            
              • Protection Paladin: Holy Shield block raised to 30%, Redoubt lowered.
                • 
            
              • Shaman totem stacking tightened; Disease Cleansing Totem and Totemic Recall fixed.
                • 
            
              • Mining: fewer poor copper veins in starter areas. Skinning: one skin per creature again.
                • 
            
              • Respec costs 1 silver during beta only.
                • 
          
              
          
              The Sept 24 build also lowered Righteous Fury's holy threat bonus to 60%, made Holy Strike's shorter cooldown baseline for Holy, and let Rejuvenation, Renew, Riptide and Wild Growth crit.
